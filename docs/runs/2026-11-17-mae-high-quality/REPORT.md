## Protocol and retained failures

The acceptance criteria were: source-correct sparse encoding and post-encoder mask insertion; contribution-led image hierarchy; isolated image-only reading followed by factual pixel review; accepted raster frozen before faithful native reconstruction; actual Office rendering/font/route checks; independent final fidelity/editability review.

A frozen narrative plan bound two version-1.1 briefs to the same scientific semantics. Two actual initial generations requested different compositions. Candidate A realized aligned sparse/full regions with a full-width loss strip rather than a wholly different orientation; B realized a continuous top bottleneck with a compact lower-right objective. Composition search produced useful variation, but did not guarantee radically different visual grammars.

Both initial images failed exact loss-selector counts. A had five blue selector positions despite four visible tokens. B's selector was only 3×4. The initial factual review did not catch the full selector problem until explicit enlarged-region counting; its corrected report and diagnostic crops are retained. A fresh isolated reader's account was saved before factual review, with neutral paths and source/brief withheld.

B was preferred for its continuous sparse-to-full architectural argument. One actual attached-image edit produced B2: a 4×4 selector with exactly four white unscored positions and twelve gold scored positions, matching the restored decoder grid, plus an exact Masked-patch MSE label. Independent source review accepted B2; a separate pixel-only rereading confirmed the main story while noting the compact lower panel. **Three ImageGen calls were used: two initial generations and one attached edit, within the four-call limit.** No discarded call, seed, model snapshot or benchmark score is invented.

B2 was frozen with hashes before native authoring. Native v1 was retained; its arrows were visibly smaller than the reference. The owning authoring source corrected arrow size in v2. No frozen target was replaced or QA threshold relaxed. The typography sample passed actual LibreOffice rendering; the full output passed measured layout, reference geometry, native XML, actual Office routes/text and actual-font coverage. Final independent review supplies the visual verdict separately.

## Scientific invariants

- The 4×4 diagram is a teaching example: 16 image-patch slots, four retained, twelve masked. CLS tokens and positional/projection bookkeeping are intentionally outside this overview, so diagram counts do not claim complete tensor sequence lengths.
- Only visible patches pass through the encoder. A shared learned mask vector is repeated twelve times after encoding; restore indices recover original patch positions.
- The lightweight decoder receives all sixteen image-patch positions and predicts all patch pixels.
- The original target supplies supervision only. A distinct binary mask selects twelve masked positions for MSE. The four unscored selector positions match the restored blue positions: r1c1, r1c4, r3c2, r4c4.
- Encoder and decoder are trained during pretraining. No frozen encoder, downstream decoder, measured reconstruction quality or performance claim is depicted.

## Verification and limits

The exact output counts are recorded in `native-audit.json`: **21 native text boxes covering 25 labels, 147 native shapes, 20 native connector segments, zero pictures/media**. There are 30 transparent attachment helpers excluded from the content denominator. Counts establish native representation, not visual quality.

Actual renderer: **LibreOffice → PDF → MuPDF**. Measured layout/native audit, reference anchors, font-style profile, rendered Arial coverage, composed rendering and route checks passed for output-v2. The five centered text blocks retained their intended line counts. No core skill implementation changed for this case; the changes are case-local authoring, evidence and an index link. The smallest sufficient verification is this real artifact/render/review run, not another unrelated unit-suite batch.

Unknown source typeface is approximated with measured Arial bold; mild gradients become source-color solid fills, as recorded by the raster reviewer and contract. Landscape microcontours and antialiasing are approximate. Orthogonal connectors are editable segments and may need manual rerouting after module moves. Interactive Microsoft PowerPoint editing and Microsoft PowerPoint rendering were not tested.

This result supports one full author-informed delivery on a visually strong original paper. It does **not** establish superior visual quality over MAE Figure 1 or reliable fully automatic design from arbitrary code. The new design is more explicit about mask/restore/loss; the author original remains more economical and visually focused. [Comparison details](AUTHOR_COMPARISON.md).

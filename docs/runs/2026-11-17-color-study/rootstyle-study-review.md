# Independent style-study and native-output review

**Verdict: accept the saved guidance and both reviewed palette variants.** This
review is bounded to the selected 2026 reference corpus, the actual local
method-page renders, the source-derived guide/assets, and two native Office
palette-only outputs. It does not make a venue-wide, award, reuse-permission,
accessibility, or publication-superiority claim.

## Source grounding and skill-pack judgment

The final index records 18 distinct papers attempted in 20 logical download
attempts: 16 inspected method/architecture figures (12 ICLR 2026, 4 CVPR 2026).
MotionStream and U-Mind each failed two bounded download attempts; UltraMemV2
and FedAFD replace them within their respective venues. The failures remain
separate from the inspected denominator. The method-page corrections are present:
LaVer Fig. 3/p. 4, MemoryVLA Fig. 2/p. 4, and MoAlign Fig. 2/p. 5 were opened.
The early LaVer chart/teaser note remains a counterexample, not method evidence.

The saved guidance translates observed visual roles into constrained choices:

- Preserve dark structural ink and neutral space when pale surfaces carry roles.
- Choose token, border, route, module surface, phase field, or local core before hue.
- Keep labels, position, direction, line style, and glyphs as redundant cues.
- Use line-first treatment where geometry already explains the relation.

This is grounded in mixed evidence: MetaEmbed/MEM1 show repeated role colors,
MemGen has phase bands, final LaVer method Fig. 3 uses peach/plum token roles
with navy/light-blue teacher-student blocks, and MemAgent is a legitimate
mostly-monochrome counterexample. The earlier LaVer benchmark chart remains an
excluded chart-specific counterexample, not the method observation. The guide
avoids a single “ICLR 2026 style,” a fixed color count, required pastel treatment,
or a source-paper reuse claim.

Four original project-authored Office-rendered previews are self-contained under
`paperfigure/assets/styles/`; their provenance records hashes and confirms no
source-paper artwork is copied. The four visual grammars are distinct and usable:
role surfaces, token continuity, overview-plus-detail, and line-first structural
ink. The line-first grammar explicitly has no claimed bundled accepted example.
The four palette families are clearly described as authored treatments, not
sampled author palettes, defaults, or accessibility certifications.
